Wonder Who He Is?

Chris Wonder is a Canadian magician who has over half a decade and counting of sleight of hand experience. He holds a BFA in Dramatic Arts.

He is the head of the Royal Canadian Entertainment Company, a magic and variety entertainment brand.

He has also:
Performed 3 straight years at the YYC Sunfest Festival.

Held monthly charity shows for the Ronald McDonald House in Alberta

Appeared at both restaurant and private events around the city as featured entertainment.

He also holds educational sessions for youth.
