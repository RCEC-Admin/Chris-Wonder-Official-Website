How much do you charge? ⏷

The fee depends on a number of variables including:
The type of magic (close-up or stage)
The size of the event
The length of performance you desire
Whether travel and accommodation are involved and so on.

The easiest thing to do is tell me about your event and I'll send you a no-obligation quote with some different options!

Do you perform for children? ⏷

I aim to entertain mainly mature audiences that being corporate and private adult events, but on rare occasions I have entertained children!

I have done festivals, local community events, and birthday parties, and been told I am quite gifted in entertaining children, though my passion lies in the more mature audiences.
